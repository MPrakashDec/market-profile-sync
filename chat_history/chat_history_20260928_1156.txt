### Meaning of **Channel 1 Depth** in Fyers Versova TBT

In Fyers' high-throughput **Versova (Tick-by-Tick / TBT)** WebSocket architecture:

---

### 1. The Multiplexed Channel Architecture
Instead of opening multiple WebSocket connections or sending all data down a single unsegmented pipe, Fyers Versova uses numbered virtual **Channels (1 to N)** to multiplex subscription traffic:
* **Channel Partitioning:** Clients allocate symbols to specific channels (`"channel": "1"`, `"channel": "2"`, etc.).
* **Channel Controls:** Channels can be paused, resumed, or cleared independently (`resumeChannels: ["1"]`, `pauseChannels: []`) without tearing down the underlying TCP/WebSocket connection.

---

### 2. Depth Mode vs. Lite / SF Mode
When subscribing on a channel, the `mode` parameter defines the payload detail:

| Subscription Mode | Packet Contents | Data Frequency / Granularity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`lite` / `sf` (Symbol Feed)** | LTP, LTP Vol, Chg, Day OHLC | 1-second sampled or tick-level basic price |
| **`depth` (Level-3 / 50-Depth TBT)** | **Full 50-Level Order Book** (Top 50 Bids + Top 50 Asks, individual price/quantity/order count per level), Total Buy/Sell Quantities (`tbq`, `tsq`), sequence numbers, feed timestamps | Continuous broadcast on every order placement, modification, cancellation, or trade match |
