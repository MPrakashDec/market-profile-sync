Antigravity consists of a user interface (running in an Electron/Chromium container) and a background service (`language_server.exe`). The UI loads the background service over HTTPS on `127.0.0.1` (localhost).

* **When disconnected:** Norton's SSL interceptor goes inactive or bypasses local loopback scanning. The correct fingerprint is passed, the app validates it, and loads successfully.
