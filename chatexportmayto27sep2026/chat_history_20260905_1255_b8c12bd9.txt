In AI research, that exact phenomenon is called:

### **"Lost in the Middle"** (or the **U-shaped Attention Curve**)

It was formally proven in a landmark research paper by Stanford, UC Berkeley, and CMU:
1. **Primacy Effect (First ~20%):** The model pays high attention to the very beginning (system prompts, initial problem definition).
2. **Recency Effect (Last ~20%):** The model pays high attention to the immediate recent messages and latest user inputs.
3. **The "Trough" (Middle ~60%):** When contexts stretch to tens of thousands of tokens, transformer self-attention mechanisms degrade significantly in the middle. Crucial instructions, nuances, edge-case constraints, and facts buried in that 60% valley are either ignored or overwritten by the model's pre-trained biases.
