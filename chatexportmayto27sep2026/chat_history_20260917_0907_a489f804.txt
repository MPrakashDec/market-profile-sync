### WHAT THE "CROSSED VWAP" ALERT SIGNIFIES

The True 09:15 Futures VWAP is the volume-weighted institutional average price for the intraday session. A crossover alert serves three operational purposes:

---

### 1. Shift in Intraday Orderflow Control (Bull/Bear Pivot)
* **CROSS ABOVE (`> 23303.5`):** Buyers have taken net control of the session's volume inventory. Statistically, long setups have positive drift expectancy toward the upper auction boundary (IB High: `23345.1 – 23350.0`).
* **CROSS BELOW (`< 23303.5`):** Sellers have displaced buyers, indicating downward inventory rebalancing toward the lower support shelf (`23236.0 – 23260.0`).

---

### 2. Identifying "Mean-Reversion Chop" vs "Trend Drift"
* **Multiple Whipsaw Crosses (e.g. between 09:41 and 09:50 IST):**  
  When you see repeated `CROSS BELOW` and `CROSS ABOVE` alerts within 5–10 minutes, it is an immediate signal that the market is in **Rotational Balance (OAIR)**.
* **The Rule:** **Never chase momentum across VWAP during a balanced bracket.** Instead, treat VWAP as a magnet/fair-value anchor where buyers and sellers are agreeing on price.
