---

### 1. The Expiry Regime: Everyday 0DTE vs. Structured Expiries
* **In the US (SPX / QQQ):**  
  There is a 0DTE expiry **every single day of the week, Monday through Friday**. Volatility tools (like Volland, SpotGamma) are built around massive intraday pin-risk and 0DTE gamma squeezes happening daily.
* **In India (Post-SEBI Regulatory Overhaul):**  
  SEBI banned multiple weekly expiries. Now there is strictly **one weekly index expiry per exchange**:
  - **NSE:** Only **NIFTY 50** has weekly expiry (Thursdays). Bank Nifty, FinNifty, Midcap weekly expiries were **killed** (they only have monthly expiries now).
  - **BSE:** Only **SENSEX** has weekly expiry (Fridays).
* **Why US Tools Fail Here:**  
  On non-expiry days (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday for NIFTY), gamma is much more dispersed across the week, and theta burn doesn't exhibit the violent 0DTE gamma explosion seen in the US every single afternoon. You need **term structure and calendar awareness**, not just a single-day 0DTE model.

---

### 2. European Style (Cash Settled) vs. American Style (Physical Delivery)
* **In the US:**  
  Equity options are American style (can be exercised anytime, physical stock assignment risk). Tools spend huge amounts of code modeling early assignment risk.
* **In India:**  
  Index options (NIFTY / SENSEX) are **European style and cash-settled**. There is zero early assignment risk. Modeling early exercise boundaries is completely useless noise in India.

---

### 3. Hedging Instrument: Cash Underlying vs. Index Futures
* **In the US:**  
  Market makers delta-hedge index options primarily using the cash ETF (SPY) or E-mini futures (/ES), but the primary reference is the cash index.
* **In India:**  
  **Indian institutions delta-hedge almost exclusively via NIFTY / SENSEX Futures!**  
  The futures trade at a **cost-of-carry / basis premium (contango/backwardation)** relative to spot. If your tool calculates Market Profile or dealer gamma against Cash Spot instead of the active Futures contract, your levels will be off by 50 to 120 points! This is why our pipeline strictly mandates **Futures-First**.

---

### 4. Severe Intraday Margin Constraints (SEBI Mandates)
* **In India:**  
  - SEBI added a **2% Extreme Loss Margin (ELM)** penalty for short options on expiry days.
  - Upfront 100% option premium collection is strictly enforced.
  - Intraday margin benefits for calendar spreads are removed on expiry days.
* **Why US Strategy Visualizers Fail Here:**  
  Tastyworks assumes US Portfolio Margin rules where you can run naked ratio spreads with tiny margin buffers. In India, running naked tails will trigger margin calls and exchange penalty freezes. Strategies must be **strictly risk-defined** (spreads, flys, condors).

---

### 5. Liquidity Clustering (Only Front Week & Nearest Round Strikes)
* **In the US:**  
  Liquid strikes exist 300 points OTM, across 10 different expirations out to 2 years.
* **In India:**  
  90%+ of all liquidity is hyper-concentrated in the **active front-week contract**, within $\pm 400$ points of spot, and clustered heavily on **round 100/500 strike numbers** (24,000, 24,100, 24,200).
