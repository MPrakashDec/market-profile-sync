To systematically read the market and execute valid setups, the framework requires seven core data layers:

---

### 1. Prior Session Structural References (Context)
Before the open, establish where the market is trading relative to prior value:
* **Key Levels:** Prior Day High (PDH), Prior Day Low (PDL), Prior Day Close (PDC).
* **Profile Value Areas:** Prior Value Area High (VAH), Value Area Low (VAL), and Point of Control (POC).
* **Opening State:**
  * *In Value / In Range:* High probability of balance/mean reversion.
  * *Out of Value / In Range:* Testing sentiment; potential responsive activity.
  * *Out of Range (Gap):* True imbalance; potential conviction drive.

---

### 2. Opening Auction Type (09:15 – 09:45 IST)
Classifies the conviction of Other Timeframe (OTF) participants in the first 15–30 minutes:
* **Open-Drive (OD):** Direct directional drive away from open with zero retest. (High conviction trend).
* **Open-Test-Drive (OTD):** Tests an immediate reference level, finds rejection, and drives aggressively the other way.
* **Open-Rejection-Reverse (ORR):** Auctions in one direction, meets strong opposing liquidity, rejects sharply, and breaks the day's open.
* **Open-Auction-In-Range (OAIR):** Oscillates inside prior range without clear directional conviction.

---

### 3. Initial Balance Metrics (09:15 – 10:15 IST)
The first hour of trade (Brackets A & B) sets the volatility baseline for the day:
* **IB High (IBH) & IB Low (IBL):** Defines the balance boundary.
* **IB Range Width:**
  * *Narrow IB (< 75 pts on Nifty Fut):* High probability of a directional breakout / Range Extension (RE).
  * *Wide IB (> 150 pts on Nifty Fut):* High probability that extreme limits will contain trade for the day (Normal / Balance Day).

---

### 4. Developing Day Type & Bracket Progression
Monitors whether the market is accepting new prices or rotating within value:
* **Day Types:**
  * *Trend Day:* One-time framing all day, IB broken early, VWAP holds without overlap.
  * *Normal Variation Day:* IB extension occurs (usually 1.5x–2x IB range) and then consolidates.
  * *Double Distribution Day:* Trend moves to a new level, then forms a second balance zone separated by single prints.
  * *Normal / Balance Day:* Prices remain within the IB.
  * *Neutral Day:* Extensions on both sides of IB, closing back inside.
* **One-Time Framing (OTF):** Consecutive 30-minute brackets making higher lows (OTFU) or lower highs (OTFD). Break of OTF signals potential balance/exhaustion.

---

### 5. Volume Profile & Tape Auction Dynamics
* **Nifty Futures True VWAP:** The primary intraday fair value benchmark.
* **Volume POC (VPOC):** Heaviest traded volume price; acts as an auction magnet.
* **High Volume Nodes (HVN):** Areas of price acceptance and consolidation.
* **Low Volume Nodes (LVN):** Areas of rapid rejection and transition.
* **Single Prints (Anomalies):** Uncontested auction zones that often get repaired during retests.

---

### 6. Options Gamma Exposure & Structure (Derivatives Overlay)
* **Call Wall (CW) & Put Wall (PW):** Largest open interest concentrations acting as structural ceilings and floors.
* **Gamma Regime:**
  * *Positive Net GEX:* Dealers buy dips and sell rips (low volatility, mean-reverting environment).
  * *Negative Net GEX:* Dealers trade with momentum (high volatility, trend continuation).
* **Strike Moneyness:** Map futures targets to closest liquid ATM/ITM option strikes.

---

### 7. Execution Invariants & Risk Rules
Every valid trade requires hard mathematical pre-conditions:
* **Strict Order Invariants:**
  * Long / Call: $\text{Stop} < \text{Entry} < \text{Target}$
  * Short / Put: $\text{Stop} > \text{Entry} > \text{Target}$
* **Risk Envelope:** Maximum 3 trades per session, strictly defined stop-loss, and mandatory 15-minute post-trade cooldown.
