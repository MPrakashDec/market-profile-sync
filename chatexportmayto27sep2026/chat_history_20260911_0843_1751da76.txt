### 1. Why Law 3 (15-Minute Cooldown) Exists in Quantitative Trading
* **The "Revenge & Whipsaw" Trap:** 85% of retail drawdown happens when a trader exits a position (especially a loss or premature exit) and immediately jumps right back into the very next candle on emotion.
* **The Regime Reset Need:** After an exit, price usually needs 10 to 15 minutes to establish a fresh auction balance, reset Delta-OI flow, and shake out weak hands.
* **The Math:** Missing 1 continuation move is vastly cheaper than getting chopped in 4 consecutive false breakout entries.

In quantitative system design, there are two distinct ways to handle this:
